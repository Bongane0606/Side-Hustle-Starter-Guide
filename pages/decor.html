Decor setup business

Weddings, funerals,
parties, and every event
in between.

This guide shows how to start event decor without buying every chair, table, tent, glass, plate, and stretch tent on day one. Start with setups you can deliver well, then build inventory from paid bookings.

What you sell

Decor is a service plus inventory

A decor setup business does more than rent items. Customers pay you to make the event look organised, match colours, deliver on time, set up neatly, collect items, clean them, and solve problems quietly.

You can serve weddings, funerals, birthday parties, bridal showers, baby showers, graduations, corporate events, church events, lobola celebrations, unveiling ceremonies, school events, and community meetings.

Starter offer example:

10-person table setup with table cloth, runner, napkins, plates, glasses, cutlery, centrepiece, candles, setup, and collection.

Customer pays deposit to book the date. Balance is due before setup.

Exact launch steps

How to start from zero

  1. Choose one event type first

    Pick the event you can understand and reach: funerals, birthdays, baby showers, weddings, or small family gatherings. One focus makes your package easier to price and market.

  2. Create a small package

    Start with a 10-person table setup or a backdrop package. Write exactly what is included, what colours are available, how long setup takes, and what the customer must provide.

  3. Make sample setups for photos

    Use your own home, yard, church hall, or borrowed table. Take clear daylight photos from the front, side, and close-up. Photos sell decor faster than long explanations.

  4. Find rental partners

    Before buying chairs, tables, tents, and stretch tents, find reliable people who already own them. Ask for their prices, delivery rules, deposit rules, and replacement costs.

  5. Set booking rules

    Use a deposit, final payment deadline, breakage deposit, setup time, collection time, and cancellation rule. Send these rules by WhatsApp before accepting the booking.

  6. Record every item

    Use a checklist for plates, glasses, chairs, cloths, poles, lights, and decor pieces. Count before delivery, count at collection, photograph damage, and charge for missing items.

Pricing

Do not quote from your head

Direct costs

  • Items bought or rented for the event
  • Transport to and from the venue
  • Assistants for loading and setup
  • Cleaning, laundry, and repairs

Business costs

  • Data and airtime
  • Storage boxes and labels
  • Marketing photos and flyers
  • Emergency replacement fund

Profit

  • Your planning and setup time
  • Money to grow inventory
  • Owner payment
  • Risk for damage and late changes

Quote formula: rental or stock cost + transport + labour + cleaning + emergency amount + profit. If the customer adds more tables, chairs, glasses, plates, tent space, flowers, lights, or stretch tent size, the price must change.

Policies

Rules protect your profit

Deposit

Take a booking deposit to secure the date. For bigger events, take enough to cover rental partners or stock purchases.

Breakage

Charge a refundable breakage deposit for glasses, plates, chairs, stretch tent parts, lights, and fragile decor.

Final payment

Require final payment before setup starts. Once items are installed, your risk is already active.
